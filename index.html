⚠️ Scope & Limitations

This tool applies the regular sound-change rules of the Korean standard pronunciation rules (표준 발음법). It works out how a written word is pronounced; it does not know what the word means. Keep these limits in mind:

  • One word at a time: each word (text between spaces) is analysed on its own. In fluent speech sounds also change across word boundaries (못 해요 → [모태요]).
  • Compound words: words made of two parts (e.g. 꽃잎, 한여름) often gain an extra sound (ㄴ-insertion or 사이시옷) that depends on the word's origin. Only the common cases listed in the tool are handled.
  • Irregular tensification: some words harden for historical reasons (e.g. 비빔밥 [비빔빱], 문법 [문뻡]). Only the listed cases are handled.
  • Verb stems ending in ㄴ or ㅁ: in verbs like 신다 [신따] and 감다 [감따] the next consonant hardens, but in other words it doesn't. The tool can't tell verbs from other words, so it leaves single ㄴ/ㅁ unchanged. Double batchim (앉다, 젊다) are handled.
  • Loanwords: words borrowed from other languages often don't follow the hardening rules (e.g. 멕시코 is [멕시코], not [멕씨코]).
  • Vowel length: long and short vowels are not shown.
